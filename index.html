Modern & Sustainable Farming Tips

Proven agronomic techniques to minimize water wastage, restore soil microbiology, curtail chemical dependencies, and boost farm profitability.

Precision Drip & Alternate Wetting

Water management is the key differentiator between profit and loss.

  • In Rice, use Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) via perforated pani-pipe to save 25-30% water without yield reduction.
  • In Groundnut, install inline drip irrigation (2.0 LPH drippers) to maintain friable soil during underground pegging.
  • Schedule irrigation during cool early mornings to cut evaporation losses.

Groundnut Pegging & Gypsum Strategy

Gypsum application is the single most crucial factor for groundnut pod filling.

  • Broadcast Gypsum @ 400 - 500 kg/ha at 40-45 DAS (flowering to peg entry).
  • Calcium directly enters developing pods, preventing "pops" (empty unfertilized shells).
  • Do not disturb soil with deep cultivators after peg initiation.

Balanced NPK & Micronutrient Priming

Overdosing with Nitrogen invites pests like BPH and Rice Blast.

  • Apply basal Zinc Sulphate (25 kg/ha) to prevent Khaira disease in paddy.
  • Split Urea into 3 doses: 50% basal, 25% at tillering, and 25% at panicle emergence.
  • Incorporate green manuring crops like *Dhaincha* (Sesbania) prior to main crop sowing.

Bio-Pesticides & IPM Strategy

Combine biological parasitoids and organic extracts for sustainable pest control.

  • Deploy yellow sticky traps @ 10/acre for thrips and aphids in groundnut and vegetables.
  • Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) at early vegetative stages.
  • Use pheromone traps for Rice Yellow Stem Borer to monitor moth surges.

Soil Health Card & Composting

Replenish soil organic carbon for sustained multi-year fertility.

  • Test soil pH and organic carbon every two years before Kharif sowing.
  • Incorporate 2 tonnes of enriched Vermicompost alongside Trichoderma culture.
  • Avoid burning crop stubble; mulching retains soil moisture and beneficial earthworms.

Smart Agri-Drones & Weather Windows

Leverage precision technology for ultra-low volume foliar applications.

  • Drone spraying cuts water usage by 80% and covers an acre within 7-10 minutes.
  • Never spray when wind velocity exceeds 15 km/h to prevent chemical drift.
  • Always check our live **Weather & Spray Advisory** widget before scheduling operations.
